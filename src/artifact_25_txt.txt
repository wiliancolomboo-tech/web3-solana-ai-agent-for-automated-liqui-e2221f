## 4.4 Run Modes
1. **Dev/Test**
   - `DRY_RUN=true`, `EXECUTION_MODE=simulate`, use Mock adapter and local test keys.
2. **Staging**
   - `DRY_RUN=false`, `EXECUTION_MODE=simulate`, real adapters + simulation-only checks.
3. **Production**
   - `DRY_RUN=false`, `EXECUTION_MODE=live`, builder endpoint or native protocol adapter with real signing.
